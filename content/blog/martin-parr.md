# Martin Parr — Short & Sweet

The first time I saw Martin Parr’s work was in Bogotá in 2019. I was fresh out of university and found his work so fun! I remembered mostly the colors, the quantity of his work, and the satirical view of tourism.

7 years later, I return by chance to his work. This time at _Fotografiska Stockholm_. The exhibition is, as its title depicts, short and sweet. A collection of his most relevant work, a huge part dedicated, of course, to modern tourism.

Age has probably made me more compassionate than I was back then. While I was at the gift store wanting to get a magnet for one of his photographs, I started to wonder about the people depicted in the pictures. Just normal people, wanting to enjoy time off with their family and the material means they had. However, in Parr’s photograph, I felt they were being made fun of, especially in his early work, _The Last Resort_, depicting the working class on English beaches.

I now enjoy his later series _Global Tourism_ more. Here, he seems more of an observer of tourism as an activity, and the silly and absurd things people do when visiting new places. Like trying to take a selfie ‘holding’ the Tower of Pisa, or huge groups of tourists posing for pictures at the Acropolis in Athens.

![Martin Parr, The Leaning Tower of Pisa, 1990](/assets/martin-parr.jpg)

As I get older, I enjoy traveling less and less. I find it rewarding, but it takes a toll on my health, energy, and the environment. I enjoy staying close to home and yes, traveling around a couple of times a year, but the hunger to explore far and unknown places is simply not there.
